What I work on

Research themes.

Hyperspectral remote sensing

Designing UAV-mounted hyperspectral imaging workflows—flight planning, radiometric calibration, and soil–vegetation spectral separation—to extract physiological signals from canopy reflectance across hundreds of narrow bands.

Machine learning for nitrogen management

Building and evaluating PLSR and Random Forest models that translate hyperspectral features into in-season nitrogen status predictions and yield estimates for snap beans and kidney beans grown on irrigated sandy soils.

Precision vegetable production

Integrating multispectral, hyperspectral, and thermal imagery with agronomic field trials to develop decision-support tools that enable variable-rate nitrogen application and reduce environmental nitrogen losses.

Geospatial foundation models

Exploring how large pretrained Earth observation models (Prithvi, AlphaEarth) can be adapted for crop-specific spectral analysis and benchmarked for site-specific agricultural management tasks.
